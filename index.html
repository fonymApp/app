SHARING YOUR OWN CARD

The code is the card.

Your QR code carries the contact itself, not a link to fetch one. Phones read it with their built-in camera app, with no app required on their end — so there is nothing to load and nothing to look up online. It works even when neither phone has a connection.

And the people you exchange cards with never hear from us. No invitations, no follow-up emails. They get your contact details, and that is all.
