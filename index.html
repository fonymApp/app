Share your card

Simple ways to share your info.

Share by QR code, AirDrop, or messaging. Most phones read your QR code with their built-in camera app, so the people you exchange cards with don’t need Fonym to receive your card.

Of course, they might decide they want a Fonym too.

Pro tip

Especially convenient at conferences and trade shows — the QR code carries your details itself, so it works even when the hall’s Wi-Fi doesn’t.

Privacy

Your contacts are yours.

Fonym was designed with privacy in mind. Your contacts, attachments, notes, and other information stay on your device. Nothing leaves your phone unless you choose to share it or back it up.

You can create password-protected backups and store them wherever you choose.

And the people you exchange cards with never hear from us. No invitations, no follow-up emails, no marketing messages. They get your contact details, and that is it.

Fonym developers do not run servers. We’re here to make the app that you enjoy using — and we’re proud to carry Apple’s “Data Not Collected” label.
